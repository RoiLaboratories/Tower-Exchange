## 1. Sources of Personal Data

We obtain personal data from the following sources:

- **Directly from you:** when you connect a compatible wallet (including an embedded wallet provisioned through our wallet infrastructure provider), create or manage a user profile, participate in affiliate or referral programs, or interact with the platform (e.g., trading activity, viewing quotes, using the AI conversational agent, or setting up recurring orders).
- **Automatically through your use of our Services:** including data collected via cookies, log files, and web analytics when you access tower.exchange or app.tower.exchange, such as transaction intent data, quote views, AI agent interaction logs, and referral tracking.
- **Third-party and partner sources:** such as aggregated DEX liquidity providers on Arc (including Xylonet, Synthra, and Unitflow), blockchain analytics tools, Circle's infrastructure (USDC, EURC, CCTP, Paymaster, and related services), analytics platforms, and performance-tracking partners.
- **Publicly available sources:** including on-chain Arc blockchain data that is publicly accessible, such as wallet addresses, transaction history, and token interactions.

## 6. Information Security

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data, including access controls, role-based permissions, encryption in transit and at rest, secure coding practices, and regular vulnerability assessments.

Because Tower Exchange operates on a non-custodial model, users remain custodians of their own wallet secrets and private keys where a self-custodial wallet is used; where an embedded wallet is provisioned through our wallet infrastructure provider, key management follows that provider's security architecture. No login credentials are stored by Tower Exchange for self-custodial wallet connections. All access to personal data is governed by the principle of least privilege, with strong authentication requirements and regular employee training on data protection.

While we take reasonable steps to protect personal data, no system is completely secure. We encourage users to safeguard the confidentiality of their own credentials and wallet private keys. We may suspend use of the Services without notice pending investigation if a security breach is suspected.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, and in particular under the GDPR, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Opt-out:** stop receiving direct marketing communications you previously consented to receive.
- **Access:** request details of how we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it.
- **Portability:** receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and request we transfer it to another controller where technically feasible.
- **Correction:** request correction of inaccuracies in your personal data.
- **Deletion:** request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances (e.g., the data is no longer necessary, you object to processing, or the data was unlawfully processed), subject to exceptions such as legal compliance obligations.
- **Object/restrict:** object to or request restriction of certain processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@tower.exchange. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
